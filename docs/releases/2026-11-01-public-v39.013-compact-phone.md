## Purpose and approval

Make the phone scoring and Stats screens easier to use during bowling while preserving tablet/desktop Full Layout. Michael reviewed the unpublished candidate in the existing fixed Pixel 7 emulator and approved publication before the production release.

## Changes

- Keep ten phone pin-count slots in fixed positions, disabling impossible counts. Put Miss, Undo, and Split in a consistent utility row; show Split state and preserve Quick/Detailed eligibility rules.
- Use full-width next-action buttons for Next Bowler, Start Next Game, or Finish Series when appropriate.
- Group Ball/Lane, entering average/handicap details, and manual game/series actions behind **Game details & actions**. Scroll the active team bowler tab into view after rotation.
- Put the phone Stats summary before a collapsed Filters panel with a visible summary of selected filters.
- Reduce phone header clutter, improve form text/touch targets, position toast notices near the top, and use a two-column scorecard/keypad arrangement on short landscape phone screens.
- Coordinate the v39.013 client, manifest, service-worker/cache, version file, and versioned assets for installed PWA updates.

## Scope and data protection

Presentation changes are guarded by Compact Phone layout. Full Layout on tablets and desktops is preserved. No scoring, handicap, statistics, saved-history schema, Google Sync policy, live-sharing policy, or telemetry policy change is part of this release. The personal bowling app remains separate and unchanged.

The existing Pixel 7 emulator uses a locked 412 × 915 CSS-pixel viewport with DPR 2.625. Preview scenarios used synthetic sample data. The installed-mode adjustment needed to show the candidate correctly was confined to the temporary QA server; it is not a production application change. The existing modified MacBook repository launcher is preserved.
