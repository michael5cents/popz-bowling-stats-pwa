# Popz Bowling - Owner-Approved Refund Policy (Commercial Release)

**Business decision approved November 15, 2026.** Owner policy is decided; final customer-facing legal review and publication are separate launch steps. No live payment acceptance is authorized by this decision alone.

## Policy

All purchases of Popz Bowling Lifetime Pro are **final**. Because Lifetime Pro is a one-time $9.99 software unlock, **we do not offer change-of-mind, non-use, or dissatisfaction refunds**. The free Pro trial is 30 days **before purchase**; it is not a post-purchase money-back period.

**Exceptions:** Contact us about an actual billing problem, such as an incorrect amount, duplicate charge, charge that was not authorized, or payment charged but the Pro purchase not correctly applied to the buyer's account. We will investigate and correct a verified billing error, including refunding an erroneous charge when appropriate. This policy does not limit any rights or remedies required by applicable law.

**Billing support:** feedback@popzbowling.com. Provide the date of purchase and Stripe receipt/reference; never share full card numbers, CVV, or account passwords. Refunds that are warranted or legally required are made to the original payment method through Stripe. Bank processing time varies.

**Disputes and entitlement:** A refund or chargeback alert triggers an operator review. It does not automatically issue a refund, revoke Pro, remove a Founder grant, delete a customer account or erase scores/history. Verified purchase activation failures should be investigated and fixed before assuming a refund is needed. Every actual refund or entitlement change requires an authorized operator decision subject to applicable law.

## Approved compact purchase disclosure

“All Lifetime Pro sales are final. Refunds are only for billing errors (such as duplicate or incorrect charges) or where required by law. Billing help: feedback@popzbowling.com.”

Display the compact disclosure in the app's purchase panel and in Stripe-hosted Checkout before the payment button. Publish the expanded policy with terms and privacy disclosures as part of the coordinated commercial release.

## Launch status

Policy decided and staged in the feature branch, **not independently reviewed for legal compliance or published** as a customer-facing policy. Live checkout and server trial enrollment remain disabled until the coordinated go-live. No automatic refund feature is enabled.
