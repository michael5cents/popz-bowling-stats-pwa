Privacy policy

Effective November 15, 2026

Bowling information on your device

Popz Bowling stores scores, leagues, bowling locations, balls, shot settings, dates and statistics on your device. These remain local unless you choose Google Sync, share a live score link, or export a backup. Browser or device data clearing may erase local records; we recommend backups.

Google sign-in and optional Sync

Google login is optional for local scoring but needed for account-based trials, purchases and Sync. Google Firebase Authentication supplies an account identifier, email and sign-in status. When you use Sync, the app saves completed bowling sessions, settings and deletion markers to Firebase/Firestore so your devices can synchronize. Firebase rules restrict ordinary users to their own data. Account-level Pro entitlements and payment references are stored to restore purchased access.

Payment processing

Stripe handles checkout and card processing. Popz Bowling records purchase references and payment status necessary to grant Pro and resolve billing errors; it does not ask for complete card numbers or CVV codes.

Live score links

Only if you start sharing, selected current-series score snapshots are available to anyone with the spectator link. Links expire and may be stopped; screenshots already seen cannot be retracted. Past history and other bowlers are not included.

Technical diagnostics

The app collects limited anonymous app use and reliability information, such as app version, platform, sanitized error information and general technical events. These reports do not include bowling scores, names, leagues or Google identities. Cloudflare and other providers may process ordinary network logs to provide and protect services.

Storage and retention

Local information remains until removed, reset or cleared by your browser/device. Cloud records and billing audits may remain for service, account restoration, security, accounting or legal purposes until deletion is requested or appropriate. We do not promise an automatic deletion schedule that is not implemented.

Your choices and requests

You may bowl locally without a Google login, export or import data, sign out, and stop score sharing. Request access, correction or deletion of account-linked data at feedback@popzbowling.com. We may verify account ownership. Deleting cloud records cannot remove external copies of backups you saved.

Service providers and changes

Cloudflare hosts the app; Google Firebase handles optional login/sync; Stripe handles payment; Telegram may relay nonidentifying billing incident summaries to the operator. The latest version of this notice is posted at popzbowling.com.
