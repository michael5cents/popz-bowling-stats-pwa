USER GUIDE

How to Use Popz Bowling Stats

A quick guide from installation through scoring, stats, backup, and feedback.

1

Install the App

Open popzbowling.com. On Android/Chrome, tap Install App. On iPhone/iPad, use Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen → Add. You can also use the app in the browser without installing it.

2

Set Your Name

Open Settings and enter your bowler name. Your data stays on this device; no account is required.

3

Start a Series

Tap Bowl. Enter the date, league or practice name, bowling center, number of games, oil pattern, entering average, handicap settings, and an optional series name. If you leave the average blank, the app uses the running average already stored for that same league or Practice when available. The default handicap is 90% of 220; change it if your league uses something different.

4

Record Ball and Lane

For each game, enter the ball used and lane number(s). The Entering / Current Average remains editable while you bowl, so you can correct a missed or wrong average and the handicap updates immediately. Previously used names are remembered so you can select them again without worrying about capitalization.

5

Enter Each Roll

Use the on-screen pin keypad after every shot. Strikes, spares, open frames, the 10th frame, scratch score, handicap, and handicap score are calculated automatically.

6

Track Leaves and Splits

After a first ball that leaves pins standing, tap the pins that remain on the pin deck and choose Save Leave & Continue. This powers your single-pin, 10-pin, split, and leave-conversion statistics.

7

Fix a Mistake

Use Undo for the most recent roll. To correct an older frame, tap that frame on the scoreboard, change the roll values, and save it. The score recalculates automatically.

8

Move Between Games

Use New Game when the next game is ready. When the bowling session is over, tap Finish Series. The app first shows a This Series results screen for only the series you just completed; choose Continue to Overall Stats for cumulative stats, or open History.

9

Use Stats

The Stats screen shows average, high game, high series, strike/spare percentages, leave conversions, and performance breakdowns. Filter by league/practice, bowling center, lane, ball, or oil pattern.

10

Review History

Tap History to see saved series and frame-by-frame game details. Your history remains local to this browser or installed app.

11

Back Up Your Data

Popz Bowling requests persistent browser storage and automatically downloads a JSON safety backup whenever you finish a series. Keep those backup files somewhere safe such as Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or another backup location. You can also use Export Backup anytime and Import JSON in Settings to restore.

12

Updating the App

Already installed? You do not need to uninstall or reinstall. Stay online and reopen the app. On Android, fully close it from Recent Apps and reopen it; if needed, open popzbowling.com in Chrome and refresh once. On iPhone/iPad, open popzbowling.com in Safari, refresh once, then close and reopen the Home Screen app. Check Settings → App Version to confirm the update. Updates replace app files only and do not erase your bowling history.

13

Feedback

To report a problem or suggest an improvement, use Email Feedback; it sends to feedback@popzbowling.com. The app version and device information are included automatically.

Privacy: Popz Bowling Stats does not require an account and does not upload your bowling scores to Popz. Your bowling history is stored on your device unless you choose to export a backup.
